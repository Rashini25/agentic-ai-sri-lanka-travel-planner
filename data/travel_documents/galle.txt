Destination: Galle

Location:
Southern Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Galle is a coastal city famous for the historic Galle Fort, beaches, and colonial architecture.

Main Attractions:
- Galle Fort
- Unawatuna Beach
- Jungle Beach
- Maritime Museum

Best Time to Visit:
December to April is suitable for beach activities.

Transportation:
- Trains and buses connect Colombo and Galle.
- Local buses and tuk-tuks are available.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can explore Galle with approximately Rs.3000-5000 per day.

Accommodation:
Budget hotels and guest houses are available around Galle and Unawatuna.

Travel Tips:
Explore Galle Fort during evening hours for a better experience.
