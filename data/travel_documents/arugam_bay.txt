Destination: Arugam Bay

Location:
Ampara District, Eastern Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Arugam Bay is a world-famous surfing destination with beaches and a relaxed atmosphere.

Main Attractions:
- Arugam Bay Beach
- Whiskey Point
- Muhudu Maha Viharaya
- Kumana National Park

Best Time to Visit:
May to October is the main surfing season.

Transportation:
- Buses are available from Colombo and nearby towns.
- Private vehicles and taxis are available.
- Tuk-tuks are common for local travel.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can explore Arugam Bay with approximately Rs.4000-7000 per day.

Accommodation:
Many budget hostels, surf camps, and guest houses are available.

Travel Tips:
Reserve accommodation early during surfing season.
