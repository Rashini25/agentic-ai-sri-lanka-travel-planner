Destination: Jaffna

Location:
Northern Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Jaffna is a cultural city known for Tamil heritage, historic sites, islands, and unique food.

Main Attractions:
- Jaffna Fort
- Nallur Kandaswamy Temple
- Delft Island
- Jaffna Library

Best Time to Visit:
May to September is suitable for exploring the northern region.

Transportation:
- Trains connect Colombo and Jaffna.
- Buses are available from major cities.
- Local transport includes buses and tuk-tuks.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can explore Jaffna with approximately Rs.3000-6000 per day.

Accommodation:
Budget hotels and guest houses are available.

Travel Tips:
Try local Jaffna cuisine and plan island trips in advance.
