Destination: Kitulgala

Location:
Kegalle District, Sabaragamuwa Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Kitulgala is an adventure tourism destination famous for white-water rafting, rainforest experiences, and outdoor activities.

Main Attractions:
- Kelani River
- White Water Rafting
- Belilena Cave
- Makandawa Forest Reserve
- Waterfalls

Best Time to Visit:
January to April is suitable for outdoor adventure activities.

Transportation:
- Buses are available from Colombo, Kegalle, and nearby towns.
- Private vehicles and taxis can be used.
- Tuk-tuks are available locally.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can visit Kitulgala with approximately Rs.4000-7000 per day depending on activities.

Accommodation:
Budget guest houses, eco-lodges, and adventure camps are available.

Travel Tips:
Book adventure activities with registered providers.
Carry suitable clothes for water activities.
