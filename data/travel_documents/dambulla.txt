Destination: Dambulla

Location:
Matale District, Central Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Dambulla is a historic city famous for ancient cave temples and cultural attractions.

Main Attractions:
- Dambulla Cave Temple
- Golden Temple
- Popham's Arboretum
- Nearby Sigiriya Rock Fortress

Best Time to Visit:
January to April is suitable for sightseeing.

Transportation:
- Buses are available from Kandy and Colombo.
- Tuk-tuks are available for local travel.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can explore Dambulla with approximately Rs.3000-5000 per day.

Accommodation:
Budget hotels and guest houses are available.

Travel Tips:
Combine Dambulla with Sigiriya for a better travel experience.
