Destination: Colombo

Location:
Western Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Colombo is the commercial capital of Sri Lanka and a major urban destination. It is known for modern city life, colonial architecture, shopping areas, cultural attractions, and coastal views.

Main Attractions:
- Galle Face Green
- Colombo National Museum
- Gangaramaya Temple
- Independence Memorial Hall
- Pettah Market
- Lotus Tower

Best Time to Visit:
January to April is generally suitable for sightseeing because of lower rainfall.

Transportation:
- Trains connect Colombo with many major cities in Sri Lanka.
- Public buses are available throughout the city.
- Colombo has taxi and ride-hailing services.
- Tuk-tuks are commonly used for short-distance travel.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can explore Colombo with approximately Rs.3000-5000 per day excluding luxury activities.

Accommodation:
Budget hotels, hostels, guest houses, and affordable apartments are available throughout Colombo.

Travel Tips:
Use public transport for budget-friendly travel.
Visit Galle Face Green during sunset.
Plan travel times carefully because Colombo traffic can be heavy.
