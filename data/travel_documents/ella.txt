Destination: Ella

Location:
Badulla District, Uva Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Ella is a famous mountain destination known for scenic landscapes, hiking trails, waterfalls, and the famous railway journey.

Main Attractions:
- Nine Arches Bridge
- Little Adam's Peak
- Ella Rock
- Ravana Falls

Best Time to Visit:
January to May is considered a good period for hiking and sightseeing.

Transportation:
- The train journey from Kandy to Ella is one of Sri Lanka's most scenic routes.
- Buses are available from Badulla and Colombo.
- Tuk-tuks are available for local travel.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can explore Ella with approximately Rs.4000-6000 per day.

Accommodation:
Many budget hostels, guest houses, and homestays are available.

Travel Tips:
Carry comfortable shoes for hiking activities.
