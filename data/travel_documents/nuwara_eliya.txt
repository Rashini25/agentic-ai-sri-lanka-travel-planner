Destination: Nuwara Eliya

Location:
Central Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Nuwara Eliya is known as Little England because of its cool climate, tea plantations, and colonial architecture.

Main Attractions:
- Gregory Lake
- Horton Plains National Park
- Pedro Tea Factory
- Lover's Leap Waterfall

Best Time to Visit:
February to April is suitable for sightseeing.

Transportation:
- Trains and buses connect Nuwara Eliya with Kandy and Colombo.
- Local buses and tuk-tuks are available.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can explore Nuwara Eliya with approximately Rs.4000-6000 per day.

Accommodation:
Budget hotels, hostels, and guest houses are available.

Travel Tips:
Bring warm clothes because temperatures can be low.
