Destination: Ratnapura

Location:
Sabaragamuwa Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Ratnapura is known as the City of Gems and is famous for gem mining, waterfalls, forests, and nature-based tourism.

Main Attractions:
- Gem Museum
- Sinharaja Forest Reserve
- Bopath Ella Waterfall
- Maha Saman Devalaya
- Adam's Peak nearby

Best Time to Visit:
January to April is suitable for outdoor activities.

Transportation:
- Buses connect Ratnapura with Colombo and other cities.
- Private vehicles and taxis are available.
- Local buses are available for nearby attractions.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can explore Ratnapura with approximately Rs.3000-5000 per day.

Accommodation:
Budget hotels, guest houses, and eco-lodges are available.

Travel Tips:
Carry rain protection because the area receives frequent rainfall.
Avoid buying gems without proper certification.
