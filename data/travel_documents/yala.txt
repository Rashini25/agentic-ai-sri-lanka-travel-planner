Destination: Yala

Location:
Southern and Uva Provinces, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Yala is famous for wildlife safaris and is one of the best places to observe Sri Lankan wildlife.

Main Attractions:
- Yala National Park
- Wildlife Safari
- Leopard Observation
- Kataragama Temple

Best Time to Visit:
February to June is suitable for wildlife viewing.

Transportation:
- Buses are available to nearby towns.
- Safari vehicles are required inside the national park.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can visit Yala with approximately Rs.5000-8000 per day.

Accommodation:
Budget camps, guest houses, and hotels are available near the park.

Travel Tips:
Book safari tours early during peak seasons.
