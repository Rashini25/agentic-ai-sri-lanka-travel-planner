Destination: Haputale

Location:
Badulla District, Uva Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Haputale is a mountain town known for beautiful views, tea estates, and hiking locations.

Main Attractions:
- Lipton's Seat
- Adisham Bungalow
- Diyaluma Falls
- Tea Plantations

Best Time to Visit:
January to May is suitable for hiking and sightseeing.

Transportation:
- Trains connect Haputale with Kandy and Colombo.
- Buses are available from nearby towns.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can explore Haputale with approximately Rs.3000-6000 per day.

Accommodation:
Affordable guest houses and homestays are available.

Travel Tips:
Carry warm clothing because temperatures can be cool.
