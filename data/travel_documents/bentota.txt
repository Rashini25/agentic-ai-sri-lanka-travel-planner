Destination: Bentota

Location:
Galle District, Southern Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Bentota is a coastal destination famous for beaches, water sports, and river activities.

Main Attractions:
- Bentota Beach
- Bentota River
- Water Sports
- Brief Garden

Best Time to Visit:
December to April is suitable for beach activities.

Transportation:
- Train and bus services are available from Colombo.
- Tuk-tuks are available around the area.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can explore Bentota with approximately Rs.4000-7000 per day.

Accommodation:
Budget hotels, guest houses, and homestays are available.

Travel Tips:
Compare prices before booking water sports activities.
