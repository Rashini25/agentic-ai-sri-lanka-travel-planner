Destination: Negombo

Location:
Western Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Negombo is a coastal city located close to Bandaranaike International Airport. It is famous for beaches, seafood, fishing culture, and lagoon activities.

Main Attractions:
- Negombo Beach
- Negombo Lagoon
- St. Mary's Church
- Dutch Canal
- Fish Market

Best Time to Visit:
December to April is suitable for beach activities and sightseeing.

Transportation:
- Trains connect Negombo with Colombo.
- Public buses are available from Colombo and nearby areas.
- Tuk-tuks and taxis are available for local transportation.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can explore Negombo with approximately Rs.3000-5000 per day.

Accommodation:
Many budget hotels, beach guest houses, and hostels are available.

Travel Tips:
Negombo is a convenient first destination after arriving at the airport.
Try local seafood dishes.
