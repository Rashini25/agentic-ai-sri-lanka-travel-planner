Destination: Polonnaruwa

Location:
North Central Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Polonnaruwa is an ancient royal city with historical ruins and UNESCO World Heritage status.

Main Attractions:
- Gal Vihara
- Royal Palace
- Parakrama Samudraya
- Ancient ruins

Best Time to Visit:
May to September is suitable for exploring outdoor locations.

Transportation:
- Buses connect Polonnaruwa with major cities.
- Trains are available from Colombo.
- Bicycles are useful for exploring ruins.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can visit Polonnaruwa with approximately Rs.3000-5000 per day.

Accommodation:
Budget hotels and guest houses are available.

Travel Tips:
Carry water because many attractions require walking.
