Destination: Sigiriya

Location:
Matale District, Central Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Sigiriya is an ancient rock fortress and UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Main Attractions:
- Sigiriya Rock Fortress
- Pidurangala Rock
- Sigiriya Museum
- Minneriya National Park

Best Time to Visit:
January to April provides comfortable weather.

Transportation:
- Buses are available from Dambulla and major towns.
- Private vehicles and taxis are available.
- Local tuk-tuks can be used.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can visit Sigiriya with approximately Rs.3000-5000 per day.

Accommodation:
Budget hotels and guest houses are available near Sigiriya and Dambulla.

Travel Tips:
Visit early morning to avoid heat and crowds.
