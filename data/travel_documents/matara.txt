Destination: Matara

Location:
Southern Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Matara is a coastal city known for beaches, historical sites, temples, and access to several popular southern destinations.

Main Attractions:
- Matara Fort
- Parey Dewa Temple
- Polhena Beach
- Dondra Lighthouse
- Star Fort

Best Time to Visit:
December to April is suitable for coastal activities.

Transportation:
- Trains connect Matara with Colombo and other southern cities.
- Public buses are available from major towns.
- Tuk-tuks are available for local travel.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can explore Matara with approximately Rs.3000-5000 per day.

Accommodation:
Budget hotels, guest houses, and homestays are available near beaches and the city.

Travel Tips:
Matara can be combined with Mirissa and Weligama for a southern coastal trip.
