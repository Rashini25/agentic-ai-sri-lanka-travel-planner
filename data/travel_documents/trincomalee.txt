Destination: Trincomalee

Location:
Eastern Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Trincomalee is a coastal city known for beautiful beaches, marine activities, and cultural attractions.

Main Attractions:
- Nilaveli Beach
- Pigeon Island National Park
- Koneswaram Temple
- Fort Frederick

Best Time to Visit:
April to September is suitable for beach activities.

Transportation:
- Trains and buses connect Colombo and Kandy with Trincomalee.
- Local buses and tuk-tuks are available.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can visit Trincomalee with approximately Rs.4000-6000 per day.

Accommodation:
Affordable hotels, guest houses, and beach accommodations are available.

Travel Tips:
Book accommodations early during holiday seasons.
