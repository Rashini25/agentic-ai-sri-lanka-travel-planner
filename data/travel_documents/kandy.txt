Destination: Kandy

Location:
Central Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Kandy is a cultural city famous for the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, Kandy Lake, traditional dancing, and historical attractions.

Main Attractions:
- Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic
- Kandy Lake
- Royal Botanical Gardens Peradeniya
- Udawatta Kele Sanctuary

Best Time to Visit:
January to April is generally suitable for comfortable travel.

Transportation:
- Train from Colombo Fort to Kandy is a popular budget option.
- Public buses are available from major cities.
- Tuk-tuks are useful for local transportation.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can visit Kandy with approximately Rs.3000-5000 per day excluding luxury activities.

Accommodation:
Budget hotels and guest houses are available around Kandy city.

Travel Tips:
Book train tickets early during weekends and holidays.
