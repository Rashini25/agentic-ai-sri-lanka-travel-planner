Destination: Mirissa

Location:
Matara District, Southern Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Mirissa is a popular beach destination known for whale watching, surfing, and coastal scenery.

Main Attractions:
- Mirissa Beach
- Whale Watching
- Coconut Tree Hill
- Parrot Rock

Best Time to Visit:
November to April is suitable for beach activities and whale watching.

Transportation:
- Trains and buses are available from Colombo.
- Tuk-tuks are available for local transportation.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can visit Mirissa with approximately Rs.3000-6000 per day.

Accommodation:
Budget hotels, hostels, and beach guest houses are available.

Travel Tips:
Book whale watching tours in advance during peak season.
