Destination: Anuradhapura

Location:
North Central Province, Sri Lanka.

Overview:
Anuradhapura is an ancient city famous for Buddhist heritage, historical ruins, and UNESCO World Heritage sites.

Main Attractions:
- Sri Maha Bodhi
- Ruwanwelisaya Stupa
- Abhayagiri Monastery
- Thuparamaya

Best Time to Visit:
May to September is suitable for exploring historical locations.

Transportation:
- Trains and buses are available from Colombo.
- Buses connect Anuradhapura with major cities.
- Bicycles and tuk-tuks are useful for exploring the ancient city.

Budget Information:
Budget travelers can explore Anuradhapura with approximately Rs.3000-5000 per day.

Accommodation:
Budget hotels and guest houses are available near the city.

Travel Tips:
Carry water and visit historical sites early morning.
